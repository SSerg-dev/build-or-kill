# Plan 01-03: Result Rendering, Markdown Export, and Eval Checks

<objective>
Complete the user-facing result experience and verification harness. This plan renders the final advisory result in the locked order, exports all sections to Markdown, and adds a lightweight eval fixture set to protect the AI contract.
</objective>

<context>
Must follow `.planning/phases/01-mvp-validation-app/01-CONTEXT.md`:
- D-10: verdict block first
- D-11: compact role cards
- D-12: section order: strengths, risks, MVP scope, validation experiments, next actions
- D-13: brief and scannable output

Must follow `.planning/phases/01-mvp-validation-app/01-AI-SPEC.md`:
- schema validity
- clarification routing
- verdict exclusivity
- grounding in provided input
- non-fabrication
- actionability
- role differentiation
</context>

<must_haves>
- D-10: The result starts with a dominant verdict block containing verdict, confidence score, and short reason.
- D-11: The five AI role analyses render as compact role cards below the verdict block.
- D-12: Remaining sections render in the locked order: strengths, risks, MVP scope, validation experiments, next actions.
- D-13: Result sections stay brief, scannable, and bullet-oriented.
</must_haves>
